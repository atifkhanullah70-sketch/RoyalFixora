How Much Does an Electrician Cost in Pakistan?

Typical electrical rates in Islamabad, Rawalpindi and Lahore range from PKR 600 to PKR 2,000 per visit, depending on the job. Simple tasks like replacing a switch or fixing a socket are at the lower end. Larger jobs — rewiring a room, installing a UPS, or setting up a full backup system — cost more. Emergency or after-hours work carries a premium.

Royal Fixora connects you with electricians who quote upfront before starting. You'll know the price before they arrive.
