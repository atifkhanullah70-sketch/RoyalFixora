How Much Does a Plumber Cost in Pakistan?

Typical plumbing rates in Islamabad, Rawalpindi and Lahore range from PKR 500 to PKR 1,500 per visit, depending on the type of job. Simple fixes like tightening a tap or clearing a drain are at the lower end. Larger jobs — replacing pipes, installing geysers, or fixing bathroom fittings — cost more. Emergency or after-hours work is usually charged at a premium.
