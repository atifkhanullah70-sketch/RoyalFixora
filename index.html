FREQUENTLY ASKED

Common Questions

Everything you need to know about booking a home service with Royal Fixora.

How do I book a home service with Royal Fixora?

Choose your service, fill in your details, and we'll connect you with a verified professional via WhatsApp. The whole process takes under two minutes.

Which cities does Royal Fixora serve?

Currently serving Islamabad, Rawalpindi and Lahore. We're expanding to Karachi and Faisalabad soon.

Are the professionals on Royal Fixora verified?

Yes. Every professional is screened for experience, background, and customer reviews before being listed.

How much does a plumber cost in Pakistan?

Typical rates range from PKR 500–1500 per visit depending on the job. Royal Fixora connects you with professionals who provide upfront quotes.

Can I reschedule or cancel a booking?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp at least 2 hours before your scheduled slot and we'll arrange it.

What payment methods are accepted?

Cash on completion is standard. Many professionals also accept bank transfer, JazzCash, and Easypaisa.

Is Royal Fixora available for commercial properties?

Yes — offices, shops, and rental properties are all supported. Contact us on WhatsApp for bulk or recurring bookings.
